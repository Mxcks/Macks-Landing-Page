PRIVACY

What happens to your details.

This page says what this site collects, what Cal.com collects when you book, and how to reach us about your details. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

What this site collects on its own

This site collects nothing on its own. There is no account to make. There is no form on this site that sends your answers to us. The site runs no analytics of its own.

The guide on the home page

The guide runs only in your browser. Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is stored by this site, and nothing is sent to us. If you copy the summary at the end, the copy sits on your device, and you choose where it goes.

When you book a call

Booking runs on Cal.com, a separate service. On the Booking Setup page, the Cal.com calendar is embedded in the page. When you book, Cal.com collects the details you type: your name, your phone number, and your email address. Cal.com holds those details in the booking.

Macks Studios receives the booking details: your name, your phone number, your email address, the service, and the time you picked. We use those details to hold the call with you and to prepare for it. We do not sell booking details.

Cookies

This site does not run advertising trackers. This site sets no cookies of its own. Booking happens on Cal.com, a separate site. Cal.com can set its own cookies, and Cal.com's own cookie rules apply there.

How to reach us about your details

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, reach us through the Book a call page. Book the free call and raise it on the call, and we will act on it.
