What happens after I book the Horizon Discovery call?

We meet for 15 minutes to inspect your intake requirements, appointment rules, and calendar setup. You receive a precise configuration document within 24 hours. Once confirmed, setup begins immediately.

Why do you prefer Zelle for payments?

Zero processor processing fees, instantaneous settlement, and simple bookkeeping. It keeps system setup costs entirely predictable for both parties without 3.5% processor drag.

Can I upgrade to Studio Foundation later?

Yes. The $500 Booking Setup applies as credit toward your $5,000 Studio Foundation. Your scheduling engine integrates smoothly into the larger architecture when you are ready.

What if my calendar is already tangled?

We audit and clean existing calendar links during the first 48 hours. We eliminate colliding schedules, clear defunct links, and align buffer times before connecting your new instance.
